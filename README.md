## Provenance and scope

The initial digest and annotated discussion history were assembled from a ChatGPT research conversation held across **October 11–12, 2026**. Later notes cover subsequent conversations and linked research projects. These are research syntheses, **not independently validated theories**. The discussion history is an **annotated reconstruction**, **not a verbatim ChatGPT data export**, and distinguishes participant statements from reconstructed summaries.

### Key distinctions

- **Historical dimension:** origins, instantiation, independent re-emergence, inheritance, and transmission.
- **Functional dimension:** recognition, response profiles, valuation, action, and feedback.
- **Regulatory dimension:** structures controlling the enactment, interpretation, and revision of other structures.

These relationships are explored across human psychology, social systems, biological coding, and chemical interactions. Analogies between media should not be mistaken for demonstrated mechanistic equivalences.
