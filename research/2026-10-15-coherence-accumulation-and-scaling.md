# Research Digest — Accumulating Coherence and the Conditions of Scaling

**Discussion:** October 15, 2026  
**Status:** Conceptual research; proposals and open questions, not an established universal law  
**Context:** [Symbolic Entity — Working Definition](symbolic-entity-definition.md)

## Central insight

> **Coherence enables scaling by making previously established relationships available as components of subsequent organization.**

An established organization can become the starting condition for further organization. The next level need not reconstruct every relationship that made the previous level possible; it can operate on their stabilized result.

This brings a previous formulation into a more general context:

> Once a reduced representation becomes stable enough to be instantiated and reused, it can participate as a single unit in higher-order structures.

The possible extension is that this applies not only to symbolic representations but to organization more broadly.

## How this line of thought arose

DNA was introduced as a **thought example**, not as a claim that undiscovered hidden codes must reside in non-coding DNA. Removing a familiar object from its commonly assigned purpose allows its structural relations, alternative interactions, constraints, and possibilities to come into view. A thing's known function does not exhaust what its structure is or what effects it may have under different conditions.

This led to a broader question: What is the coherence that **glues things together or keeps things apart**? Coherence may enable combinations and also exclude incompatible arrangements. Boundaries are part of what should be investigated, rather than assumed to be extrinsic limitations.

The simplest entry point is repetition. **A repetition alone is already a reason to look for a rule.** Strictly, the repeat is an observation of regularity; an underlying rule is a hypothesis to be tested. No presumed meaning, purpose, or conscious interpreter is needed to begin.

## Four investigative operations

| Operation | What it exposes | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repetition** | A recognizable recurrence or regularity | Recurrence alone does not establish why it happens |
| **Variation** | What can change while some relation remains recognizable | Perturbations must be specified, not merely imagined |
| **Persistence** | Relations or arrangements that survive relevant variation | Persistence need not imply permanence or reproduction |
| **Failure / separation** | Conditions and boundaries beyond which a pattern no longer persists or interacts | Failure need not reveal one sharp threshold |

This is a **research heuristic**, not a universal algebra or a direct derivation of Noether's theorem.

## Coherence, accumulation, and scaling

A provisional progression is:

1. **Regularity:** Relations recur.
2. **Stabilization:** Some relations persist across relevant variations.
3. **Accumulation:** Stabilized structures alter the possibilities and constraints for subsequent interactions.
4. **Scaling:** A larger process can treat an existing organized structure as a unit.
5. **Higher-order organization:** Relations become possible *between* such units.

These are **conditional transitions**, not an inevitable sequence. Repetition can also destabilize, exhaust, destroy, or prevent an organization.

The key claim is qualitative, not simply quantitative:

> **Accumulated coherence changes what can subsequently exist.**

Established coherence can both *enable* and *constrain*. It may shorten the work needed to establish the next structure by embodying relationships that otherwise would have to be produced anew. This is a connection to **compression**: an effective higher-level unit makes a lower-level organization available without restating all its internal relations.

### Preservation versus reduced constructive work

A productive distinction emerged:

- **Preservation** helps explain why an arrangement remains available.
- **Reduced constructive work** may explain why that arrangement can support scaling.

The second remains a hypothesis, and "work" would need precise operational meaning (energy, computation, search effort, assembly steps, or some other cost) before it can be measured across domains.

## Two roles of repetition

**Exposing possibilities.** Under existing constraints, repeated events and perturbations can reveal persistent relations or stable configurations.

**Changing possibilities.** In systems with feedback and memory, recurring processes may modify their own conditions: a flow may reshape a channel; repeated activity may alter future responses. Such effects can be called **recursive stabilization**, but they are not automatic consequences of repetition.

The proposal "repetition discovers the possible and entrenches the actual" is a useful shorthand **only for systems in which those two processes demonstrably occur**. It should not be treated as a universal law.

## Relation to symbolic entities

The existing working definition distinguishes an instantiated pattern, its physical carrier, a compatible mechanism, and the resulting effect.

This digest proposes distinguishing three research levels:

- **Underlying coherence / relational conditions:** constraints and compatibilities under which structures can form, persist, combine, or separate.
- **Instantiated symbolic entities:** reproducible patterns with structural identity that can act through compatible mechanisms, as specified by the working definition.
- **Historical accumulation:** feedback and inheritance through which stabilized structures become resources or constraints for subsequent organization.

**Do not equate coherence with a symbolic entity.** A physical regularity or law need not itself be an encoded, instantiated symbolic pattern. Also, a symbolic entity does not have to be copied onto a second carrier before it counts as instantiated; carrier independence describes the reproducibility of its type, not an additional origin threshold.

A single substrate may be compatible with multiple mechanisms, producing different causal effects. It may also carry overlapping patterns. These are distinct possibilities; an arbitrary decoder's ability to impose an interpretation is not proof of a previously organized symbolic layer.

## Boundaries and unresolved distinctions

- **Coherence is not merely "anything that has an effect."** The project should seek identifiable relationships with predictive consequences.
- **Repetition and coherence are not identical.** A pattern can recur without becoming self-sustaining; a coherent configuration can also be transient or singular.
- **Scaling is not guaranteed.** Higher levels can fail, collapse, or erase previously established structures.
- **Emergence is not established just by a change of vocabulary or scale.** Explain what new effective relations, constraints, or predictive variables appear.
- **Cross-domain analogies are probes, not proof.** Music, DNA, chemistry, and social organization need not share one physical mechanism.
- **Isomorphism of observer and observed** is a hypothesis, not established by the fact that an observer recognizes repetitions.

## Research questions

1. Can coherence be identified *before* its downstream effects and used to predict both compatibility and incompatibility?
2. What persists across variation: a material configuration, an invariant relation, a response profile, or some combination?
3. Under what conditions does an organized process become a reusable unit at a higher level?
4. Can the reduction in constructive work afforded by an existing coherent unit be measured?
5. Which processes *expose* pre-existing structural possibilities, and which *change* the landscape through feedback?
6. How can a symbolic entity be distinguished from the more general coherence that permits its existence?
7. How do breakdowns and incompatible combinations reveal organizational boundaries?

## Working proposition for future refinement

> **Coherence accumulation hypothesis:** When an organization persists sufficiently to become available to other processes, its established relationships can serve as new constraints and capabilities. Subsequent organization can then operate on that result rather than reconstructing every underlying relation. Under suitable conditions, this supports scaling and the emergence of higher-order relations.

This is **a proposed explanatory direction**, not yet a demonstrated universal principle.
