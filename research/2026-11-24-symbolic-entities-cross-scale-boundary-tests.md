# Symbolic Entities Across Scales — Comparative Cases and Boundary Tests

**Date:** 24 November 2026  
**Status:** Working research comparison; not a demonstration of a universal natural class  
**Origin:** November 2026 discussion, returning from Manager Theory to the earlier virus comparison  
**Related:** [Symbolic Entity — Working Definition](symbolic-entity-definition.md) · [Refining the Symbolic Entity](2026-10-11-symbolic-entity-refinement.md) · [Scaling and Accumulated Coherence](scaling-and-accumulated-coherence-working-definition.md) · [Cultural Development](symbolic-entity-cultural-development.md) · [Active Symbolic Constellations](2026-11-24-symbolic-constellations-perspectives-correction.md)

## 1. Why test beyond human culture?

The Manager Theory discussion focused on human hosts enacting inherited, invited, or competing symbolic patterns. The research abstraction is broader. The author proposes a collective category embracing **nonliving constructs that can influence living systems without themselves being living agents**, including tiny and extremely large organizations. The goal is to see whether the *same precise distinction* applies across scales rather than simply calling every influential thing a symbol.

That breadth is a **research ambition**, not yet a sufficient definition: gravity, sunlight, a rock, and an ordinary toxin can affect life without obviously being symbolic entities. Nor must a compatible host be alive; a computer executing a program is a comparative case.

Retain the existing working definition:

> A symbolic entity is an instantiated, reproducible pattern whose structural relations can persist as a type across carriers, and whose structure can cause effects when it encounters a compatible mechanism.

Three things must remain separate: **pattern/entity**, **carrier and compatible machinery**, and **enactment/effect**. What an enacted pattern *causes* does not establish what the pattern *is*.

## 2. Comparison matrix

The table reports **provisional classifications under the working definition**, not that every item is empirically proven to belong to one naturally unified category.

| Candidate | Pattern / candidate identity | Compatible machinery | Preliminary status and difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Viral genome** | Particular DNA/RNA sequence and functional organization | Cell plus viral-encoded functions where present | **Strong case.** Biological code is distinct from cellular translation and replication; do not equate genome with the entire physical virion. |
| **Bacterial plasmid** | Reproducible DNA organization including origin and any encoded functions | Host-cell replication/expression machinery | **Strong case.** Genomic persistence and host dependence are experimentally tractable; a plasmid is not an independent living cell. |
| **Executable program** | Ordered instructions under a specified execution convention | Hardware, runtime, OS, interpreter/compiler as appropriate | **Strong case.** The same source may behave differently under different compatible or incompatible implementations. |
| **Musical score or encoded composition** | Reproducible structural relations of pitches, durations, etc. | Musicians, instruments, playback or readers | **Strong candidate.** Distinguish score, performed sound, listener response, and culturally recognized work. |
| **Legal text or specified procedure** | Reproducible linguistic/procedural rule structure | Human interpretation, institutions, enforcement and coordination | **Strong candidate for the encoded text/rule;** its real consequences depend on practices and authority; a whole institution is not automatically one entity. |
| **Social role or ritual** | Reconstructible script of expectations and actions, if identifiable | Socially trained managers and groups | **Conditional.** A role label or repeated behavior alone is insufficient to identify the encoded pattern. |
| **Complex organization or civilization** | Potentially stable arrangements among multiple roles, rules, institutions | Many people, infrastructure, organizational processes | **Unproven as one entity.** It may be an interacting collection; a new higher-order entity needs its own reproducible organizing invariant. |
| **Prion conformation** | Templated protein conformation propagating without nucleic acids | Compatible protein substrate and biological conditions | **Hard boundary case.** Tests whether conformational propagation qualifies as *encoding* distinct from ordinary physical templating. |
| **Ordinary receptor ligand or toxin** | Molecular structure interacting chemically with a target | Receptor or other chemical targets | **Negative-control candidate.** Compatibility and causal effect alone would over-include molecular chemistry. |
| **Stone, sunlight, gravitational field** | Physical objects, energy, or regularity without an identifiable encoded and reproduced symbolic pattern | Physical systems | **Outside the current definition** merely by causing effects; no encoding has been established. |

**Biological anchors.** Standard accounts distinguish viral genetic information from the protein synthesis and other functions supplied by cells; plasmid replication similarly uses cellular machinery. Prions exhibit templated conformational propagation in the absence of nucleic-acid genomes. These empirical observations **motivate** the comparison, but do not establish that all examples instantiate one philosophically meaningful class. See the sources below.

**Virus qualification:** Whether a virus counts as living is not settled by this taxonomy. The **genomic pattern** is the principal candidate here. Delivery structures and physical replication processes also matter but must not be silently collapsed into the pattern.

## 3. Small, large, and complex are different axes

An entity's **encoded complexity**, **host dependency**, **distribution across hosts**, **scale of consequences**, and **duration** are separate dimensions.

- A short instruction may require a sophisticated host; apparent simplicity of the encoded pattern does not imply simplicity of its enactment.
- A small, reproducible sequence can produce widespread consequences when copied and enacted by many compatible hosts.
- A large institutional structure can consist of many distinct entities; size of organization alone does not confer a single symbolic identity.
- A high-order encoded arrangement can regulate the enactment of subordinate patterns, but a collection of coactive patterns does not automatically become another symbolic entity.

The [accumulated-coherence framework](scaling-and-accumulated-coherence-working-definition.md) proposes a way new levels become effective through stabilized relationships and operational compression. That does **not** by itself prove the result is symbolic. The higher-order structure must independently meet the conditions for a symbolic entity.

## 4. Five tests applicable across media

For each candidate, identify a specific instance rather than comparing broad categories such as *virus*, *religion*, or *civilization* as if each were one entity.

1. **Instantiation and identity.** What actual organized pattern exists? How is it distinguished from accidental similarity, a mere function, or the observer's description?
2. **Type persistence.** Which structural relations would be preserved across copies, alternative carriers, or reconstruction? A second copy need not actually exist, but the persistence criteria must be intelligible.
3. **Separation and compatibility.** What belongs to the pattern, what to its carrier, what to the enactment machinery, and what to the resulting behavior? Is the effect conditional on an identifiable compatibility relation?
4. **Discriminating variation.** Can changes to the pattern be distinguished from changes to the host or environment? Would controlled alterations predictably change enactment, and could an ordinary physical-interaction explanation account for the same observations?
5. **Higher-order identity.** If the case is claimed to be one entity above smaller constituents, what reproducible organization persists at the higher level? What does that classification explain that a simple collection of parts would not?

These questions are a **research protocol**, not five independently verified axioms. A case that fails or cannot answer them should remain unresolved rather than be included simply because it affects life.

## 5. The prion and ordinary-molecule challenge

The virus analogy originally motivated replacement of anthropomorphic *reading* with **compatibility-dependent enactment**. But compatibility alone is too permissive: enzymes, receptors, catalysts, and toxins also have shape-dependent effects.

Prions sharpen this boundary. Their conformations can propagate by templating a compatible protein substrate without conventional DNA/RNA coding. Two alternative resolutions must be considered:

- **Broad encoding:** a prion conformation is a reproducible structural specification enacted by a compatible substrate, and may qualify as a subclass under carefully specified criteria.
- **Narrow encoding:** its propagation is an ordinary physical templating process, not a symbolically separable code; the class boundary excludes it even though it is a remarkable self-propagating structure.

Both remain research options. No single word such as *information*, *pattern*, *recognition*, or *template* should settle the difference. Distinguish the physicochemical structure from any separately addressable or reproducible code, and ask whether the proposed identity persists under a change of carrier or mechanism.

An ordinary ligand–receptor interaction supplies a negative control: if the same criteria classify every binding molecule as a symbolic entity, the abstraction has lost its intended discriminating force. The earlier [refinement record](2026-10-11-symbolic-entity-refinement.md) already recognizes this difficulty.

## 6. The social-scale challenge

Human enactments are not uniquely privileged examples, but they offer another stress test: the same environmental event can activate different roles for different Managers, and the public explanation of an action may differ from its operative function or from a participant's private objective.

Take a formal rule, a role learned through affective expectations, and an institution that coordinates many roles:

- The **rule's encoded statement** may qualify as a symbolic entity.
- The **role as a reliably reproducible script** may qualify if its structure and transmission can be identified.
- The **institution** may be an organized host/network that enacts many symbolic entities; it is not automatically a further symbolic entity.
- A higher-order **governing charter or reproducible institutional operating pattern** may be a distinct candidate if its organizing structure persists as a type.

This connects the present tests to [Manager and self](2026-11-24-manager-self-and-governing-symbolic-entities.md) and to [symbolic constellations](2026-11-24-symbolic-constellations-perspectives-correction.md). In neither biology nor society should **host persistence**, **symbolic-pattern persistence**, and **persistence of the function produced by enactment** be confused.

## 7. How the comparison could fail

Useful counterevidence would include:

- The supposed entity cannot be distinguished from the host's ongoing physical organization.
- Reproduction or recurrence is explained entirely by a law or generic templating without an identifiable encoded invariant.
- A change attributed to an encoded pattern is actually attributable to altered machinery, resources, or physical conditions.
- A purported higher-order entity yields no additional stable identity or discriminating consequences beyond its components.
- A claimed cross-scale equivalence works only by applying different definitions in different domains.

Conversely, a convincing cross-scale comparison should specify *one shared relation* while keeping the substrate-specific mechanisms separate. Similarity of consequences is not enough.

## 8. Relations to existing research and next step

This file does **not** amend the core definition, establish all nonliving influences as symbolic, or replace the distinction between symbolic pattern, physical carrier, and enacted process. It extends the prior virus comparison and the question of higher-order scaling into a common **boundary-testing protocol**.

The immediate research task is to select one **strong positive** (e.g., a particular viral genome or executable program), one **difficult edge** (e.g., prion conformational templating), and one **negative control** (e.g., ordinary receptor binding), then explicitly apply the five tests. Afterward, compare a particular institutional rule with the combined institution to ask what, if anything, gains an independently reproducible higher-order identity.
