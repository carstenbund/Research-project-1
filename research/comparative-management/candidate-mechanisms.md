# Candidate abstract mechanisms

**Status:** All eight candidates are open. None is adopted merely by appearing here. Compare the [existing human stewardship account](../2026-11-24-manager-self-and-governing-symbolic-entities.md) before changing definitions.

## CM-01 — Implementation independence
The Manager may be an implementation-independent function coordinating resources, knowledge, dependencies and actions relative to persistence and objectives. Determine the minimum sufficient functions; distinguish a shared function from superficially similar behaviour. No assertion of corporate consciousness or that persistence always dominates other commitments.

## CM-02 — Externalization
Management functions may be instantiated in rules, institutions, software and collective procedures. Determine what can be transferred and what depends on the original host, and distinguish pattern from its enactment machinery and effects.

## CM-03 — Recursive management
First order: manage resources, commitments and actions. Second order: examine and revise methods of observation, evaluation, decisions and learning. Third order: examine and revise objectives or governing principles. Test whether these are distinct levels or existing symbolic-governance mechanisms described differently. No homunculus or infinite regress required.

## CM-04 — Proportionality of knowledge acquisition
Knowledge reduces uncertainty but consumes surplus. Preliminary investigation ratio: expected value of additional decision-relevant information / investigation cost. Investigate when improved decisions justify expenditure; stop when further information is unlikely to change an acceptable decision. Use ranges and explicitly consider catastrophic or non-negotiable consequences. Not a validated measure.

## CM-05 — Relative dependency exposure
A dependency is not inherently a weakness. Separate benefit, exposure, control, replaceability, time sensitivity, and mitigation expense. Preliminary models over a common horizon and comparable monetary units:
- Net value of accepting dependency: V_d = B - C - E (benefit less ongoing cost and expected loss).
- Mitigation ratio: R_m = (E_0 - E_1) / C_m (avoided exposure per mitigation expenditure).
- Continuity gap: max(0, replacement time - maximum tolerable interruption), with explicit contingency assumptions.

These are illustrative economic relationships; they cannot override safety, rights or regulatory constraints, nor capture all forms of value. Unknown exposure is not zero. Compare ranges, correlations, tail risk and the value of investigation. A low-materiality assumed or discovered dependency may be accepted without exhaustive investigation.

## CM-06 — Allocation of surplus to future freedom
Production (current capability), exploration (future capability and information), protection (exposure reduction), and autonomy (alternatives and reversibility) compete for scarce surplus. Investigate the conditions under which these allocations sustain persistence at different horizons; consider maintenance and reserves explicitly. Candidate for simulation, not a new variable by default.

## CM-07 — Governance as a consumer of surplus
Governance and the management of management require resources. Preliminary net value: avoided loss + improved decision value - governance expenditure, avoiding double counting. Test whether governance can become self-undermining by consuming disproportionate capacity. Prefer a compact governance core, selectable assessment instruments and independent detailed methodologies.

## CM-08 — Ownership as control over unforeseen futures
Investigate ownership and other control rights as authority over decisions not specified in advance; distinguish legal authority from practical capacity. Compare formal exit rights with actual ability to withdraw, and viable alternatives with merely theoretical ones.

## Cross-level conflict

Managers may be nested or interacting rather than centrally arranged. Objectives at individual, department, organization and wider-system levels may differ. A locally successful persistence strategy can degrade shared enabling resources; a contribution must be evaluated for obligations and options it leaves behind, not gross output alone. Assess against the existing surplus and Gravity accounts before proposing new machinery.

## Admission test

Identify a specific mechanism, a credible route of enactment, distinguishing evidence and a case where existing explanations are insufficient. Record counterevidence and scope limits. Reject, revise, or promote only after comparative review.
