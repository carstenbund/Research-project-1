## Qualifications and cross-links

- EQ-10: organizational slack may enable developmental space; see the [Garden of Eden note](../2026-10-25-garden-of-eden-recursive-enabling-environments.md).
- EQ-17: compressed indicators may conceal costs and dependencies; compare societal valuation and shortcuts.
- EQ-27: not every investment is a Leap. Retain the existing criterion of a move resting entirely on assumptions not provable from existing empirical data.
- EQ-31–40: distinguish dependency (required contribution), vulnerability (consequence of withdrawal), and autonomy (available alternatives). A dependency can increase capability while reducing autonomy.
- EQ-41–50: distinguish the symbolic pattern, carrier, execution machinery and effects. The corporation as a whole is not automatically one symbolic entity.
- EQ-51–60: investigate whether managerial evaluation, methods and purpose can themselves become objects of management without positing an additional inner supervisor.
