# Scaling and Accumulated Coherence — Working Definition

**Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Status:** Working definition / research hypothesis  
**Related:** [Coherence Accumulation and Scaling — Research Digest](2026-10-15-coherence-accumulation-and-scaling.md) · [Symbolic Entity — Working Definition](symbolic-entity-definition.md)

## Definition

Accumulated coherence is the process by which a stabilized pattern of relationships becomes available as a single, reusable component for higher-order organization.

When lower-level relationships achieve sufficient stability to persist together, a subsequent level of organization does not need to reconstruct or manage the internal interactions that produced them; it can operate directly on their consolidated result. While structural preservation explains how an organization persists, it is the **reduction of work** required to establish subsequent organization that enables a system to scale.

A compact formulation is:

> **Coherence enables scaling by compressing established relationships into stable units, reducing the work required to construct higher-order organization.**

## The Scaling Progression

The proposed transition from recurring events to multi-scale organization follows five distinct stages:

1. **Regularity.** Specific relationships recur across time or space, establishing a correlation between occurrences that can be distinguished from noise.
2. **Coherence.** Recurring relationships lock into a mutually reinforcing configuration capable of persisting across variations.
3. **Accumulation.** The established configuration alters local boundary conditions, constraining certain interactions while making others more probable.
4. **Scaling (Compression).** The entire coherent configuration is integrated as a single effective unit within a larger arrangement, eliminating the need for the higher level to explicitly represent or recalculate its internal relationships.
5. **Emergence.** Interactions among these compressed units produce new relational properties that cannot be meaningfully described at the level of the original components alone.

## Core Properties of Accumulated Coherence

Where accumulation succeeds, the resulting hierarchy exhibits the following characteristics:

- **Operational compression.** A higher level treats a complex network of lower-level relationships as one addressable unit. The consequences of the lower-level interactions are embodied in the unit's behavior rather than carried forward as an explicit description.
- **Reduction of constructive work.** By stabilizing internal degrees of freedom, an established unit lowers the energetic, informational, or combinatoric cost required to assemble the next level of structure.
- **Categorical elevation.** What functions as an ongoing dynamic process at one scale becomes a static building block (a token) at the next. Accumulation does not merely add quantity; it changes what categories of structure can subsequently exist.
- **Role of symbolic entities.** Symbolic entities represent a specialized form of operational compression: rather than transporting the full physical apparatus of a coherent system, they preserve only the lightweight relational invariants needed to re-instantiate or trigger that organization wherever a compatible mechanism exists.
- **Conditional stability (Dependency).** Accumulation is neither inevitable nor permanent. Because higher-order structures operate on the compressed results of lower levels without directly managing their foundations, they remain vulnerable to the decay, conflict, or failure of the underlying coherence on which they depend.

## Scope and questions for testing

This is a **proposed conceptual mechanism**, not a demonstrated law applying to all physical, biological, computational, or social systems. In particular:

- **Stability does not always imply mutual reinforcement.** Some persistent arrangements are maintained by external constraints or continued energy input.
- **Compression here is operational, not necessarily literal data compression.** A higher level may use the effective behavior of a lower-level unit while its internal dynamics continue.
- **Reduction of work is the central explanatory hypothesis.** The relevant cost—energetic, informational, computational, combinatorial, or constructive—needs to be specified and measured for each case.
- **Categorical elevation does not make a lower-level process literally static.** It may be *treated as a unit* on the timescale and at the resolution of the higher-level description.
- **Emergence needs a criterion.** New higher-level variables or interactions should yield distinctive explanatory or predictive value rather than simply new terminology.
- **Symbolic entities remain a distinct category.** Not every coherent structure is symbolic; the separate working definition requires an instantiated, reproducible pattern and compatibility-dependent causal effects.

A practical research question follows: **Under what conditions does treating an existing coherent structure as a unit measurably reduce the work of constructing the next level, and which failures expose the dependencies that this compression conceals?**
